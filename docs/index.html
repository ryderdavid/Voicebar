Your wallpaper, your colors

Built for talking

Say it the way you'd say it

Nobody talks in finished sentences. Voicebar hears the ums, the restarts and the pauses to think, and types what you meant.

graphic_eq

Words land as you speak, then tidy up

Your words go straight into the text box while you talk. Pause, and they're polished in place: punctuation, capitals, and the ums taken out. Change your mind mid-sentence and it keeps the change.

so um let's do tuesday no wednesday at like nine arrow_forwardLet's do Wednesday at nine.
pause_circle

Pause to think. It waits.

A pause isn't the end of a sentence. Voicebar tidies what you've said so far without adding a period or a capital, and carries on when you do.

I was thinking we couldmaybe bring the dog

Fix last dictation, by voice

Got a word wrong, or edited it by hand? Hold Voice and say what's wrong: "broad should be lowercase." Those words are instructions, never typed, and the whole message is fixed in place.

swipe_right

Tap to finish. Swipe to send.

Tap the pill when you're done. Swipe it right to finish and send, or left to throw it away. Before anything is sent, it gets one last read for what pauses leave behind, like a stray "okay" at the end.

Say the formatting

"Title case… end title case." "Quote… end quote." "Open paren… close paren." The pill shows a badge while it's on.

When you'd rather type

Everything else is one tap away

Its own keyboard, under your thumbs

ABC pops up a full keyboard over the bar, with the bottom row where your thumbs already are. Pull the handle down to put it away.

  • swipe_rightSwipe to type, run on the phone with FUTO Swipe's open models. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • keyboardHold ABC to hand the field to Gboard. A button over Gboard brings you back, already listening.

A trackpad for the cursor

Drag to move the real cursor. Hold still, then drag to select. Select all, cut, copy and paste sit right above it.

  • touch_appKeep one finger down while another drags to go further.
  • micTap Voice from the trackpad and you're dictating at the cursor.

Keys you choose

Beside ☰, Cursor, ABC and Voice, three keys are yours (one on the floating pill): Space, Backspace, Enter, Paste, Undo, Select all, Last dictation, Fix last dictation, or a switch between docked and floating.

  • touch_appHold one, or swipe up from it, to pick another on the spot. Picking only sets it; press it to use it.
  • backspaceAs Backspace, tap then press and hold to keep deleting.

One menu, one tap away

☰ opens a short list: undo, cut, copy, paste and select all across the top, then Paste with a peek at what's on the clipboard, copied in any app.

  • content_pasteRecent dictations: tap one and it's cleaned up again and copied.
  • swipe_rightSwitch between docked and floating, or to another keyboard, from the same list.

Float it, or shrink it to one button

Choose the floating style and the bar becomes a small pill: ☰, Cursor, ABC, Voice and one key you choose. From ☰ it shrinks to just the record button.

  • touch_appDrag the record pill anywhere. The app gets the whole screen, and it comes back where you left it.
  • micTap it to talk: it grows into the recording pill right there. Its ☰ brings the whole pill back.

How it works

From your voice to the text box

Each part is a choice you make once in setup. Defaults work if you never open Settings again.

mic

You talk

Tap Voice, or set it to listen as soon as a text box opens. With a keyboard attached, it listens only when the phone is in your hand.

graphic_eq

Speech becomes text

Live words from ElevenLabs Scribe, or NVIDIA's Parakeet running on the phone with no network at all.

It gets tidied up

At each pause, a fast, cheap model polishes what you said, fitted to the text around the cursor and, if you allow it, the names on screen.

Claude HaikuGPT-6 LunaOptional

send

It lands in the box

Right where your cursor is, fitted to the sentence around it. Swipe to send it with the app's own Send button.

offline_boltAn hour of talking to Scribe costs about $0.39, and a cleanup a tiny fraction of a cent. Voicebar shows you the estimate, counted on the phone.

Privacy

Where your words go is up to you

There's no Voicebar server and no account. Audio goes only to the speech service you chose, or nowhere at all.

key

Your keys, your accounts

Your ElevenLabs and Claude or OpenAI keys are encrypted with an Android Keystore key and kept out of backups.

smartphone

Or keep it on the phone

Parakeet turns speech into text on the phone. Swipe typing and the "voice bar" wake word never leave it either.

person

Only your voice

Optional filters on the phone silence other people, the TV and small children's voices before anything is sent. Your voiceprint never leaves the phone.

lock

Passwords stay out

Listening never starts by itself in a password field, and what's in one is never read for context.

Set up

Ready in a few minutes

A setup guide walks through each step, one page at a time, and checks it when you come back.

  1. Download the APKFrom the latest release on GitHub.
  2. Install itOpen the file, and allow your browser to install apps when Android asks.
  3. Turn it onTurn on Voicebar and Gboard, switch to Voicebar and allow the microphone.
  4. Add a key, or don'tAn ElevenLabs key for live speech, or download Parakeet instead. A Claude or OpenAI key turns on cleanup.
